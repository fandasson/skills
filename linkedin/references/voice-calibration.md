# Voice calibration

This calibration is distilled from the author's own posts. Match it instead of defaulting to generic "LinkedIn best practices."

- **Opening line:** Use a scene, a blunt question, or a number. Never start with a warm-up such as "I'm excited to share..." or "Let's talk about...". The first line should work even if nothing after it gets read.
- **Sentence rhythm:** Mix normal sentences with short, sometimes fragmented ones for punch. "By an AI agent. Correctly. Pushed. Done." reflects the intended rhythm. Use it as seasoning, not the entire post.
- **Numbers over adjectives:** Prefer "52 projects, 24 in production" to "a lot of projects succeeded." Put relevant numbers early rather than burying them.
- **Personality:** Self-deprecating asides, cultural references, and dry humor are welcome when natural. Do not force them into every post or scrub them out when present.
- **Endings:** End quietly with a reframe, soft invitation to think differently, or one-line implication. Never use "Thoughts?", "Agree?", or "Let me know in the comments below."
- **Grammar:** Small imperfections are acceptable when understandable: missing articles, slightly off prepositions, or imperfect tense. Do not polish the writing into flawless textbook English.
- **Punctuation:** Never use em dashes. Use periods, commas, or a new sentence instead.
- **Hashtags:** Use at most one, and only when it is genuinely useful for search. Default to none.
- **Emoji:** Use sparingly when they improve scanning, never decoratively, and never more than one or two per post.
- **Lists:** Use lists whenever they clarify the content, not only for step-by-step instructions.
- **Length:** Let the depth of the idea determine length. Do not pad a post merely to make it seem substantial.

Replace this file when adapting the skill for a different author. Base its guidance on that person's actual writing rather than generic style advice.
